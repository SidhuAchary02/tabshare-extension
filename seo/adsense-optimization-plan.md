# AdSense Optimization Plan - TabShare

Last updated: 2026-05-13

## 1) Goals

- Pass and maintain AdSense policy compliance (especially low-value-content risk).
- Improve RPM without hurting UX.
- Keep Core Web Vitals healthy while running ads.
- Build long-term trust signals (E-E-A-T style quality cues).

Success targets (first 60 days):
- AdSense approval and no policy warnings.
- Keep bounce rate stable or improved after ad rollout.
- Keep CLS under 0.1 on ad-supported pages.
- Increase page depth (pages/session) via internal linking and related posts.

## 2) Site readiness checklist (pre-approval)

Before applying or re-applying:
- Ensure all main pages are complete and indexed: `index.html`, `about.html`, `contact.html`, `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `blog/index.html`, and all 6 blog posts.
- Keep navigation clean and crawlable across desktop/mobile.
- Ensure contact and legal pages are prominently linked in header/footer.
- Confirm original, useful long-form content on homepage and blog posts.
- Remove any placeholder or thin sections that look unfinished.
- Verify `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` are reachable in production.

## 3) Ad placement strategy by template

Use a conservative first rollout. Add ads in stages.

### Homepage (`index.html`)
- 1 ad after hero + trust/value section.
- 1 ad mid-page after core educational section (for example, after features/how-it-works).
- Optional 1 ad near bottom before final CTA.
- Avoid showing ad before user sees meaningful content.

### Blog post pages (`blog/*.html`)
- 1 ad after introduction (after first 2-4 paragraphs).
- 1 ad in-content around 40-60% scroll depth.
- 1 ad near end before related posts or CTA.
- Keep at least one substantial text section between ad units.

### Legal pages (`privacy.html`, `terms.html`)
- Either no ads, or max 1 ad near bottom.
- Prioritize trust and readability over monetization here.

### 404 and utility pages
- Avoid aggressive ad layouts.
- Keep focus on navigation recovery and user intent.

## 4) Content-to-ad ratio guidelines

- Keep visible primary content above the fold before first ad.
- On long blog posts, target approximately 1 ad per 500-900 words initially.
- Keep total ad count moderate; avoid crowding on mobile.
- Never place ads in a way that mimics navigation or CTA buttons.

## 5) UX + Core Web Vitals protection

Ad layout shifts are a common revenue killer and policy risk.

Implementation rules:
- Reserve fixed-height ad containers before ads load.
- Use CSS min-height for each ad slot by format.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold ad slots.
- Do not inject ads at top after content paints.
- Keep script execution deferred where possible.

Recommended ad slot classes:
- `.ad-slot-inline` for in-content ads.
- `.ad-slot-sticky` only if it does not obstruct content.
- `.ad-slot-footer` for low-priority placements.

## 6) Mobile-first ad policy

- Minimum tap safety around ads (avoid accidental clicks).
- Do not stack multiple ads without content in between.
- Avoid large sticky ads that reduce readable viewport area.
- Test on narrow screens to ensure no overlap with nav or CTA.

## 7) Trust and E-E-A-T signals to support AdSense quality

Required trust elements:
- Strong About page with product purpose and owner/team context.
- Clear Contact page with working email.
- Detailed Privacy Policy and Terms (already improved).
- Author attribution on blog posts.
- Last updated dates on key pages.
- Internal links to related high-quality guides.

Recommended next upgrades:
- Add editorial policy section (content process and update cadence).
- Add evidence-based references in educational posts.
- Add testimonials or usage proof where available.

## 8) Policy safety controls

- Prohibit prohibited content categories and ad-policy conflicts.
- Keep autogenerated/thin pages out of index until improved.
- Do not place deceptive labels near ads.
- Do not ask users to click ads.
- Review Google Publisher Policies monthly.

## 9) Rollout phases

### Phase 1 (Week 1)
- Add 1-2 ad slots on homepage.
- Add 2 slots per blog post template.
- Keep legal pages ad-light.

### Phase 2 (Week 2-3)
- Add third in-content slot to best-performing blog posts only.
- Tune placements by scroll-depth and engagement.

### Phase 3 (Week 4+)
- A/B test ad density and position on top pages.
- Keep UX guardrails (CLS, bounce rate, time-on-page).

## 10) Monitoring dashboard (weekly)

Track by page template:
- Impressions
- CTR
- RPM
- Viewability
- Bounce rate
- Average engagement time
- CLS and LCP

Decision thresholds:
- If bounce rate rises sharply after ad insertion, reduce above-the-fold ad pressure.
- If CLS exceeds 0.1, increase reserved slot height and defer lower slots.
- If CTR is low but engagement is high, test slot position rather than adding more ads.

## 11) Recommended implementation tasks in this repo

1. Standardize ad container markup in shared templates.
2. Reserve per-slot dimensions in `assets/css/styles.css`.
3. Add lazy-loading behavior for below-the-fold slots in `assets/js/main.js`.
4. Keep one ad-slot include pattern per page type for consistency.
5. Validate desktop + mobile rendering before publishing.

## 12) Pre-launch QA checklist

- All ad slots render without overlap.
- No ad appears before meaningful content on mobile.
- No CLS spikes when ads load.
- Footer legal/contact links are visible.
- No broken links in nav/footer/blog related-posts.
- Sitemap and robots available at production domain.

## 13) Post-launch optimization cadence

- Week 1: monitor daily for policy and UX issues.
- Week 2-4: optimize placements weekly.
- Month 2+: optimize monthly, tied to content publishing schedule.

## 14) Risk controls and fallback

If policy warning arrives:
- Reduce ad density immediately.
- Remove ads from affected page types.
- Improve content depth and intent match.
- Re-submit for review after fixes.

If UX degrades:
- Remove highest-position slot first.
- Increase spacing around ads.
- Delay non-critical ad scripts.
