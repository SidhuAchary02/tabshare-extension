Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

1. Overview

TabShare is designed to make tab sharing simple and privacy friendly. This policy explains what information may be processed and how it is handled.

2. No Account Requirement

TabShare does not require signups or logins for core functionality. You can use the extension without creating an account.

3. Data Used for Sharing

When you choose to share tabs, relevant tab metadata and URLs are used to generate a shareable link experience. This is necessary to provide the feature.

4. Temporary Link Behavior

Recently shared links are intended to be temporary and auto-expire. This reduces long-term persistence and supports safer sharing practices.

5. Security and Retention

We aim to minimize data retention and follow practical safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we continuously improve protections.

6. Third-Party Services

If third-party infrastructure is used to provide extension features, those services may process limited operational data under their own policies.

7. Policy Changes

We may update this policy as TabShare evolves. Updates are posted here with a revised effective date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@tabshare.link.
